How This Simulation Works

When you enter a city name and date, the simulation automatically resolves its geographic coordinates, detects the local timezone, and downloads hourly weather data (GHI, DNI, DHI, and ambient temperature) from Open-Meteo (with automatic 7Timer! live weather backup).

At each time step, it computes the Sun's position, transposes horizontal irradiance onto the tilted or tracked panel plane, applies glass reflection losses, and calculates cell temperature via the NOCT model. These values feed a calibrated single-diode model to find the Maximum Power Point (MPP), followed by standard system losses (8%) and inverter efficiency to integrate daily AC energy.
