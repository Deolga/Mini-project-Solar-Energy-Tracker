Research Basis

Spencer (1971) & Cooper (1969) Solar Geometry

Standard astronomical solar declination and Equation of Time formulations used to compute solar elevation, zenith, and azimuth angles throughout the 24-hour cycle.

Mayer & Gróf (2021)

Extensive Comparison of Physical Models for Photovoltaic Power Forecasting, Applied Energy, 283, 116239. Supports the physical model chain involving irradiance, transposition, optical reflection, temperature, PV performance, and inverter modeling.

Copper, Sproul & Jarnason (2016)

Photovoltaic Performance Modelling in the Absence of Onsite Measured Plane of Array Irradiance and Module Temperature, Renewable Energy, 86, 760–769. Supports transposing horizontal weather irradiance to POA irradiance and estimating cell temperature.

Terminology & Abbreviations

Solar Radiation

GHI — Global Horizontal Irradiance (W/m²)

DNI — Direct Normal Irradiance (W/m²)

DHI — Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance (W/m²)

POA — Plane-of-Array Irradiance (W/m²)

Albedo — Fraction of solar radiation reflected by the ground (0.20 default)

Photovoltaic System

PV — Photovoltaic

MPP / MPPT — Maximum Power Point / Tracking

DC / AC — Direct Current / Alternating Current

NOCT — Nominal Operating Cell Temperature (45 °C default)

STC — Standard Test Conditions (1000 W/m², 25 °C)

PV Module Specs & Angles

Voc / Isc — Open-Circuit Voltage / Short-Circuit Current

Vmp / Imp — Voltage & Current at Maximum Power

Solar Elevation — Angle of the Sun above the horizon

Solar Azimuth — Compass direction of the Sun (0° N, 90° E, 180° S, 270° W)

Incidence Angle — Angle between direct sunbeams and the panel surface

Important Note

Weather data is retrieved automatically via the Open-Meteo Forecast/Archive APIs with 7Timer! live cloud/weather backup for your chosen city and date. If offline or blocked by a local firewall, the simulation smoothly falls back to a clear-sky physical estimate. Actual field PV output may still vary due to local shading, soiling, wind cooling, snow, and wiring losses.
